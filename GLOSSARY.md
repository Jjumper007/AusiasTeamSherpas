# Sherpas · Plan in Motion

A personal video per adult that explains their Sherpas financial plan, made from the plan's data and four survey answers, for families who will not meet an advisor.

## Language

### Plan and people

**Case**:
One family's plan as the challenge provides it: financial health, investments and persona.
_Avoid_: Client, account, file

**Member**:
An adult of a case who gets their own video; children only appear in their parents' stories.
_Avoid_: User, person, customer

### Personalisation

**Survey**:
The four questions a member answers before their video is made: nickname, explain level, top of mind and decision style.
_Avoid_: Questionnaire, form, onboarding

**Explain level**:
How technical the video is: keep it simple, straight talk or nerd mode.
_Avoid_: Depth, tone, mode

**Decision style**:
How the member makes big money decisions; it picks the call to action and overrides the persona's D5.
_Avoid_: Persona type, CTA type

**First step**:
The one action from the plan's top moves that the video and the card ask the member to take.
_Avoid_: Next step, recommendation, task

### Making a video

**Run**:
One pass of the pipeline for one member and one set of survey answers, with everything it produced.
_Avoid_: Job (the in-memory handle of a run in progress), generation, render

### Demo

**Demo**:
The whole member experience, from intro to video, played with demo videos instead of new runs; the same locally and at the public link.
_Avoid_: Mock, fake mode, showcase

**Demo video**:
A video made earlier by a full run, checked against its case, and played in the demo without making a new one.
_Avoid_: Pre-made video, canned video, fallback video

**Demo answers**:
The survey answers a demo video was made with; in the demo they are the only answers that can be chosen.
_Avoid_: Forced answers, preset answers

**Prototype intro**:
The first screen of the demo, which says it is a working prototype and that the demo plays a video a real run made.
_Avoid_: Disclaimer, splash screen, banner

**Run replay**:
The short wait in the demo that walks through the steps of the demo video's real run and says it is a demo.
_Avoid_: Fake loading, simulated progress
